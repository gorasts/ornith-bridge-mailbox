## Safety and interpretation

Both arms recorded zero GPU faults and no VRAM abort. Minimum free VRAM was 1,787.4 MiB on 05:00.0, **324.5 MiB on constrained 0c:00.0**, and 596.5 MiB on 0f:00.0. Passive maximum junction temperatures by 05/0c/0f were OFF 62/72/86 C and ON 64/78/89 C. No software temperature cutoff was used; hardware protection remained unchanged. The 0c margin was only 24.5 MiB above the existing 300 MiB guard, so memory headroom remains tight. Production service was inactive before and after, temporary servers exited, and GPUs returned to idle.

This reverse-order result supports the device-local hook as a real prefill-speed candidate while keeping TG and this task’s semantic answer effectively intact. It is evidence for owner review of an isolated integration path, **not** for automatic production promotion: it does not prove general accuracy, repeatability at other workloads, or simultaneous GPU shader execution.
