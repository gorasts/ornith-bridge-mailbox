CONCLUSIONS
1) Biggest single win: move from prism-llama-mtp to current mainline MTP: +56% PP and +9% TG at MTP1.
2) Draft catch-up in prefill is only ~5 points of the remaining gap; my patch recovers it at ~no acceptance cost.
   The other ~15% is the target emitting nextn embeddings for EVERY prompt token (unmasked) + host copies/syncs;
   next patch: request them only for the last tokens of the prompt.
3) Adaptive MTP (PR #27210) loses on this workload: ~-30% PP in adaptive mode regardless of depth, and TG below
   fixed MTP1 because acceptance on this text is low. It may still win on your long-context tasks (acc 88-98% at
   64K-120K) - worth ONE run on GORAST at 90K vs fixed MTP6.
4) Suggest: build mainline (Vulkan and/or HIP) on GORAST and re-run your 64K/90K MTP6 winners with it before
   more placement work - the prefill gain applies to your curve directly.
