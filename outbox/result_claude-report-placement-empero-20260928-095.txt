=== 1) PLACEMENT RECIPE (new, works) ===
Whole-layer split -dev Vulkan0,1,2 -ts 0.8,1,1 + move just enough EXPERT layers to CPU with
-ot "blk\.(L1|L2..)\.ffn_(up|down|gate)_exps\.=CPU" (layers taken from the x1 cards). Each extra CPU layer ~ -2% TG.
Keep ~0.3-0.5 GB free per card, else the prompt spills (PP 100-250; TG unaffected).
Fails: experts-only offload to x1 cards (net loss), --n-cpu-moe with a layer split (takes main-card layers -> x1 card overfills,
2-GPU PP 9-10 t/s), ub1024 (steep long-ctx decay), ub3072 (spills), q4 KV only helps where it removes a spill.
