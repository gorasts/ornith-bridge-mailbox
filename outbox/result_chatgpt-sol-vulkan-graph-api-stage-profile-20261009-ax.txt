## Source trace and attempt

That callback submits the transfer context, records graph nodes via `ggml_vk_build_graph`, ends command buffers in `ggml_vk_ctx_end`, and submits through `ggml_vk_submit` to `vk::Queue::submit`. Existing fence waits are conditional on serialized submissions; the optional Vulkan perf logger adds waits and was not enabled.
