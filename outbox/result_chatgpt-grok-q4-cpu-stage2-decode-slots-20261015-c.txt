SOURCE PROOF
Narrow is not known at slot acquire. It is known at graph_compute, after the copy has already taken a slot. That is early enough to wait, copy, and release before the inline graph reads the destination. It is not early enough to skip the acquire unless a size proxy is used instead of the graph test.

ggml-cpu.cpp:259-273. A graph is wide only when some node is at least 256 KiB. One-token decode nodes are under that, so decode takes the inline path.
