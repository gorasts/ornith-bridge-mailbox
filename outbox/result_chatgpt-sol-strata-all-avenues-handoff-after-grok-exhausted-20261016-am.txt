## Sequential avenues and limits

1. **Mirror correctness first.** Resolve static parity before AJ/AK or more performance work. Static equality would isolate an adaptation-dependent difference, not prove the adaptive math wrong or establish semantic accuracy.
2. **True MTP OFF, conditional.** Review AJ/AK arrivals again before duplicating work. Source must support a narrow non-speculative serving path with no drafter allocations. Only about159MiB remains on HIP0 after675MiB of draft experts were moved to host. Extra expert slots may not repay the loss of roughly2.2 accepted tokens/window. Broad rewrites are a source blocker, not permission to implement a new decoder.
3. **MTP relocation, conditional.** Verify `Verifier.final_R_all` and native head pointer/device ownership, host staging costs and helper headroom before code. No cross-device pointer reuse, duplicate large head, or assumption that x1 transport is cheap.
4. **Primary safe ring/gather, highest-evidence prefill avenue.** K measured0.630s actual gather span versus5.739s outside-kernel gaps. Removing all gaps is only an upper bound: about204.75→242.8 PP. P proved naive deferred flush unsafe with the96-slot ring. A future bounded lease/event design must demonstrate immutable slot lifetime and forward progress before a CPU/synthetic gate and one7501/512 pair. Do not revive compact I: PP+0.42%, TG−4.26%, first token difference40 and no net VRAM saving.
5. **True pipeline feasibility before implementation.** Current TP1 is expert parallelism on one x1 peer. A real layer pipeline needs stage-owned dense/state/scratch, bounded microbatch handoffs and a changed RAM-mirror ownership model. Budget expert-cache displacement and two/three-stage x1 transfer before building. AH explicitly refuses layer split. Claude's Q3 Vulkan gains do not predict Flash-Next HIP gains. No unsafe host-set GPU wait/event.
6. **MMQ/HC and useful grouping.** Main GU/down buckets total about13.87s; optimize a measured kernel/row layout with numerical proof, not the mislabeled dequant bucket. Gather arithmetic alone has only about1.7% PP ceiling here.
7. **Decode after mirror.** Separate CPU arithmetic from helper launch, kernel, readback and finish waits. Helpers already overlap CPU work, and remote expert optimization already reduces returns. Avoid redundant overlap implementations and driver-starving host polls. Then consider placement by time saved/MiB, CPU grouping and draft acceptance/cost.
8. **Representative validation.** A safe winner gets512-output task/retrieval checks, then32K,64/90K and120–126K prompt tests with one chat template and room for output. Record PP, TG512, both TTFT definitions, residency, KV traffic, native IDs, semantic score, faults and memory. No19-task sweep without a quality reason.
9. **Practical TTFT.** Persistent mirror amortizes its42-second copy. Prefix/KV reuse is useful but must be reported separately from cold PP. Main int8 KV is about247.5MiB plus33MiB draft; Q4 does not free multi-GiB here. Historical single-GPU U/W/X/Z results remain separate controls.
