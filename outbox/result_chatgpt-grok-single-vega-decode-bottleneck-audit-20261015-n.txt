DIAGNOSIS
On one Vega 64 the 512-token decode is about 410 to 427 ms per verify round, and about 2.3 tokens are committed per round. Roughly 70% of that round is dispatch jobs for experts that missed the GPU cache and ran on the CPU. The GPU expert math is about 57 to 62 ms inside that same pool time, not an extra term. MTP is on and is accepting drafts. It is not the reason the round is slow.

The counters below are one critical path. Pool, dispatch, and pool-multi are the same pool. Gate/up and down sit inside GPU run. File-read milliseconds and the adaptive-tier milliseconds overlap the round. They are not added.

WHAT MTP DID
Both single-GPU runs loaded the draft layer and verified windows. --spec 3 is the MTP depth. With the default suffix drafter the verify cap becomes spec+2, which is why the line says rounds of 5. The offered drafts are about 2 per round, so the MTP window stayed at 3. Acceptance 0.65 and 0.69 is real speculation, not a configured flag that never hit. Suffix added 6 windows on L and 8 on M. A low-acceptance label does not fit. The draft's own 675 MiB sits on the same 8 GB card as the expert cache, which is why the cache is only 1.56 to 1.62 GiB after weights, KV streaming, and the 700 MiB reserve. How many of the 287 ms would move if those 675 MiB were given back to the cache was not measured.
