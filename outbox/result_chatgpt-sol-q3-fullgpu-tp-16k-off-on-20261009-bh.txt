## Safety and limitations

Passive peak junction temperatures were 59/67/71 C OFF and 69/85/94 C ON for BDF 05/0c/0f. No software temperature cutoff was used; no explicit throttle indicator was sampled. The comparison is one OFF-first/ON-second pair. Process order, persistent shader-cache effects, and hardware state can influence a single pair; the size of this observed PP gain should be confirmed before treating it as repeatable. TG did not regress in this pair. The different output lengths are close but not identical. Grouped replay/self-sync is verified, not shader-level overlap. This does not establish the author's 8-GPU multiplier or predict performance at 32K/90K.
