Method. Kernel span is cudaEventElapsedTime on m.cs from the event recorded immediately before gather_native_group to the event recorded immediately after it returns. One copy16_group_kernel sits between those events. Fold runs only after the existing layer sync and the final sync. Host enqueue is CLOCK_MONOTONIC from just before the start event to just after the end event, summed over the 7085 launches. It is not GPU time and is not added to 6369.2 or to the wall.

Coverage. 7085 successful group launches, zero fallback gather_native, zero SDMA memcpys, zero pool overflows, zero non-finite elapses. Peak live events in one layer before fold: 117, under the 1024 pool. That is 7085/96 = 73.8 launches per MoE layer-pass. A full MMQ_GROUP of 16 would be about 5 launches per pass at that expert count. The count matches one launch per primary expert. Source allows that: release_to() flushes an open group when the next ring seq id was not routed, before 16 experts accumulate.
