(c) Ranked bottlenecks from measurements already taken. These are host samples and API timings, not VkQueryPool shader intervals.
1. Replay does not keep three different UBs computing at once. On the 390 tok/s self-sync-removed run, gpu_busy_percent once per second: 7 of 82 samples had two cards at or above 30%. On the slash transfer-queue run: 11 of 69. The common sample is one card near 100% and the other two at 0.
2. CPU memcpy into the pinned arena sits on the calling thread inside set_tensor_2d_async. The next card is not submitted until that copy returns. GPU work per group finishes before the next card's kernel is queued.
3. Split 0 still runs and synchronizes during capture before diagonal replay of splits 1..N. Log showed 18 splits and 7 saved ubatches, groups=3. Full three-GPU overlap is impossible during that capture.
4. Public TP global phase waits match hazard B. This port's GROUP_REPLAY path is per group H2D+COMPUTE+STORE, with ggml_backend_synchronize on the device about to be reused. That is a real dependency for the previous D2H, and it also idles that card before the new upload.
