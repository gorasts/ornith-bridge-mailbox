## Work performed

- Read original container mounts, systemd units, runtime state, disk labels/UUIDs, mount tables and bounded error logs. No replacement identity or empty data directory was created.
- Mounted Cavka drives38/3/8 read-only using ro,noload for inspection. A clean filesystem header did not establish integrity: later full kernel evidence showed faults.
- On drive38, unmounted the diagnostic mount and performed a normal original-fstab mount/start. After observing checksum errors, stopped the node, disabled its restart policy, reinstated its stopped guard and remounted it read-only. No further writable recovery was attempted.
- Drive3's guarded recovery stopped before startup because existing kernel write-I/O faults were found. Drive8 was never started.
- ASUS Storj5 received one operator start attempt and was stopped after corruption became evident. Its startup logs establish that merely restarting cannot provide stable service.
- No fsck repair, chkdsk, formatting, forced filesystem mount, piece deletion/quarantine, broad USB reset, disk-letter reassignment or reboot was performed. Existing healthy nodes and VPS routing remain unchanged.
